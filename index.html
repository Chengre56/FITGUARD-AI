Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about how FitGuard AI works with your clothing brand.

Why do apparel stores get so many sizing returns in the first place?

Online shoppers cannot try garments on in person, and static size charts with generic inches or centimeters are confusing and tedious. Because of this hesitation, shoppers often do what the industry calls "wardrobing" or "bracketing"—ordering both a Medium and a Large with the deliberate intention of shipping one back. FitGuard AI eliminates this hesitation right on the product page.

How does the 3-day free trial work?

When you start your 3-day trial, you receive 100% full access to all features, unlimited sizing calculations, and the Supabase cloud-synced merchant dashboard. You will not be charged a single cent during your 3-day trial period, and you can cancel anytime with a single click inside your dashboard.

Will the FitGuard script slow down my Shopify or WooCommerce store?

Not at all! Our client-side script is under 9KB gzipped and loads asynchronously from a global edge CDN. It performs all biometric interpolation calculations in under 10 milliseconds directly on the shopper's device with zero cumulative layout shift (CLS 0.00).

Can I have different sizing tables for different clothing types?

Yes! You can configure specific matrices for Boxy Tees, Slim-fit Hoodies, Denim Jeans, or Heavy Outerwear. The engine dynamically accounts for the distinct drape and fit profile of each garment category.
